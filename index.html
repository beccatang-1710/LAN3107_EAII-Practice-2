Task

You are on the public relations team of Earthwise Action, a non-profit organisation working to protect the environment. The organisation is going to host a workshop to educate the public about green living. You and your colleagues are going to hold a meeting to discuss the arrangements for the workshop. You should:

  • give your own suggestion(s) on the agenda items
  • explain the reason(s) for your suggestion(s)
  • listen actively and respond to others' suggestions by expressing preferences, agreement, or disagreement
  • reach a consensus on agenda items 2–4

As a group, you must cover all the agenda items below.

Meeting Details

Earthwise Action Public Relations Team 16 October 20-- at 2:30 p.m. in the Meeting Room

Agenda

  1. Opening the meeting
  2. Promotional strategies for promoting the workshop
  3. Green living tips to cover in the workshop

    Possible practices to cover:

    • Minimise energy consumption
    • Reduce paper usage
    • Use public transport
    • Conserve water
  4. AOB

    Souvenirs for participants

    This agenda item will be revealed at the assessment venue in the EAII.

    Possible souvenirs:

    • Recycled tote bags
    • Reusable food wraps
  5. Closing the meeting

Workshop Planning Meeting

A group discussion on promotional strategies and green living tips for an upcoming public education workshop.

Language Features

A ✗ marks the incorrect form (e.g. can + bought), which should be the bare infinitive (e.g. can buy).
Contractions: Let's = let us, That's = that is, I'm = I am, We've = we have, I can't = I cannot.

Communicative Functions

Communicative functions (e.g. making suggestions, showing agreement, giving reasons) are highlighted in the italic blue line next to each speaker's turn.

Cohesive devices / Linking words

  • Besides / Moreover / Furthermore (dated)
  • In addition / Additionally
  • Therefore / Thus / Hence / So

Contractions

  • I'm (I am) / It'll (It will)
  • We've (We have) / Let's (Let us)
  • That's (That is)

Thinking words (air fillers)

Well…  Ummm…  uhhhh…

Modal verbs

Ability: can, could
Possibility: may, might
Permission: Can, May
Obligation: must, have to
Prohibition: Can't, Mustn't
Offers: Will, Would
Suggestions: Shall, Should
Requests: Could, Would
Future Possibility: Will, Shall
Past Probability: Could have, Would have

+ action verb (bare infinitive) + object (noun clause), e.g. They can effectively attract younger audiences.

Grammar and Sentence Patterns

Subject-verb agreement

✗ Does everyone has a copy of the agenda?
✓ Does everyone have a copy of the agenda?

Making suggestions (action verbs: suggest + advise + recommend)

✗ I suggest / advise / recommend to print our workshop logo.
✓ I suggest / advise / recommend printing our workshop logo.
✓ I suggest / advise / recommend that you (should — British style) print our workshop logo.
✗ How about print out workshop logo?    ✓ How about printing our workshop logo?

Sentence structures

  • Cause & effect: Since / Because / As + S + V + O, S + V + O.
  • But / However + S + V + O.
