Lesson 2

Making Small Talk — Hobbies & Leisure Activities

Learn how to start conversations about hobbies and leisure activities

Useful expressions for casual small talk about hobbies, pastimes, and free time activities.

1. Starting a Casual Conversation (Icebreakers for Leisure)

The Universal Starter:

"How was your weekend? Did you get up to anything fun?"

Progress / Practice:

"How's your [hobby/practice] going? (e.g. guitar practice, marathon training)"

General Leisure Inquiries:

"What do you usually like to do to unwind after work / on weekends?"

Recent Pastimes:

"Have you had a chance to [activity: go hiking / see that new movie] lately?"

2. Active Listening: Showing Interest (Short Tokens & Echo Words)

Short Reaction Tokens

"Really?" | "Oh yeah?" | "Wow!" | "That's great!" | "That sounds fun / amazing!" | "That's impressive!"

Echo Word Formula

Pattern: "[Keyword]? That sounds [adjective]!" or "[Keyword]? That's a great way to [benefit]!"

Examples

Outdoor / Travel:

A: "I went hiking in Sai Kung."
B: "Sai Kung? That sounds refreshing!"

Creative Arts:

A: "I took up photography recently."
B: "Photography? That sounds fascinating!"

Health / Wellness:

A: "I've started doing yoga every morning."
B: "Yoga every morning? That's a great way to start the day!"

3. Giving Positive Comments & Compliments

"That's an excellent way to spend your weekend and recharge."
"Wow! Training for a marathon takes real dedication. I'm sure you'll do great!"
"I also love acoustic guitar. It's so soothing after a long day."
"That sounds really therapeutic—I definitely need a creative hobby like that!"

4. Follow-Up Questions & Intonation Guide

Rule:

  • Use Rising Intonation (↗) for Yes/No & Echo questions (warm, curious).
  • Use Falling Intonation (↘) for Wh- questions (polite, natural).
Question Type Example in Hobbies / Leisure Context Intonation
Echo Question (Auxiliary) A: "I am running a 10k race." →
B: "Are you? That sounds challenging!" 		Rising (↗)
Echo Question (Auxiliary) A: "I've just bought a road bike." →
B: "Have you? That's fantastic!" 		Rising (↗)
Echo Question (No Aux.) A: "I love playing board games." →
B: "Do you? That sounds entertaining!" 		Rising (↗)
Echo Question (No Aux.) A: "I ran my first marathon." →
B: "Did you? That's impressive!" 		Rising (↗)
Wh- Question "Where did you go hiking on Saturday?"
"What kind of music do you play?" 		Falling (↘)
Yes/No Question "Do you enjoy spending time outdoors?"
"Have you read any good books lately?" 		Rising (↗)
Question Tag "It's a really relaxing hobby, isn't it?"
"You don't play tennis, do you?" 		↘ Agree / ↗ Ask

5. Transitioning & Recommending Pastimes

"Speaking of [pastime]..."

"Speaking of outdoor activities, have you ever hiked the Dragon's Back trail?"

"By the way..."

"By the way, there's an art exhibition at the cultural museum this weekend that looks fun."

"That reminds me..."

"That reminds me, I've been wanting to try bouldering. Do you know any good climbing gyms?"

Tailored Suggestion:

"If you like strategy games, you'd really love Catan—it's so engaging!"

6. Ending the Conversation Politely

"It was great chatting about your hiking trips! We should organize a hike together sometime."
"I'd love to chat more about your photography, but I've got to run to my next meeting. Catch you later!"
"Well, it was lovely catching up. Have a great weekend enjoying your guitar practice!"

Lesson 2 Activity

Describing a Hobby or Leisure Activity

Write 30-50 words to describe your favourite hobby

Use adjectives to enrich your description. All descriptive adjectives in the model samples are highlighted to demonstrate how to enrich your writing.

Instructions:

  • Write 30-50 words to talk about your favourite hobby, leisure activity, or pastime.
  • You can make up information or quote your real experience.
  • Use adjectives from the list: active, calming, enjoyable, therapeutic, entertaining, exciting, fulfilling, refreshing, relaxing, rewarding, creative, invigorating.
  • Use the samples below to help you.

Model Samples

1. Hiking / Countryside Walking

Hiking in the countryside is my absolute favourite pastime because it is both refreshing and peaceful. Last Saturday, I hiked the Dragon's Back trail. The coastal views were breathtaking, and the cool mountain air was truly invigorating. It is a fantastic way to unwind after a busy week.

(48 words)

2. Street Photography

I took up street photography last year, and it has been an incredibly creative and fulfilling hobby. Exploring vibrant city streets with my camera is always fascinating. Capturing candid moments makes the whole pastime deeply rewarding and memorable.

(40 words)

3. Acoustic Guitar

Playing the acoustic guitar is a deeply calming and therapeutic pastime for me. After a hectic day, practicing simple fingerpicking songs feels very soothing. Learning new chords is sometimes challenging, but mastering a complete song is immensely satisfying.

(40 words)

4. Morning Yoga

I practice yoga four mornings a week. It is an enjoyable and restorative activity that keeps me physically flexible and mentally focused. The gentle stretches are remarkably calming, providing an energizing boost before work.

(41 words)

5. Board Gaming

Hosting board game nights with my friends is my favourite social pastime. It is always lively, entertaining, and full of laughter. We especially enjoy strategy games because they are intellectually stimulating and engaging. It is a wonderful way to connect.

(41 words)

6. Artisanal Baking

Baking sourdough bread on Sunday mornings is a delightful and hands-on hobby. Kneading dough is surprisingly therapeutic, and the aroma from the oven is completely mouthwatering. Sharing warm, crispy loaves with family makes it an exceptionally gratifying pastime.

(41 words)

7. Distance Running

I have been training for a 10-kilometer race, which is an active and exhilarating sport. Although long-distance training can be physically demanding and exhausting, crossing the finish line feels completely triumphant and empowering.

(39 words)

Your Turn

Write your own 30-50 word description below. Remember to highlight and underline your adjectives!

Lesson 4

Share your opinions on a new company policy

Expressing opinions

I think / feel / believe / reckon / love…
In my view / opinion, …
On the one hand, … On the other hand, …

Stating causes & explaining effects

  • Causes: …because / since / as…
  • Effects: …so… / As a result, … / Therefore / Consequently, …

Sharing personal experiences

My position is that…
Personally speaking, …
My personal feeling is that…
My own experience is that…

Giving examples: For example / instance, …

Examples

Work From Home (WFH) Policy

Positive (Alex Lam):

I think it's a very positive step forward because it gives me more flexibility and a better work–life balance. Saving more time on travelling is a huge plus and Work From Home days are generally less stressful. I should also have more time for personal activities after work so it can enhance my overall well-being.

Concern (Jacob Wong):

It seems to be increasingly popular in many companies. I think it can improve productivity for some colleagues. However, I'm worried that it might be challenging to communicate with my team members on work-related matters. It's important to have proper communication channels when we work from home to ensure effective teamwork.

Professional Development Policy

Positive (Charlie Chan):

Offering free online courses and training workshops will allow us to improve our skills without any additional costs. Plus, it's a great way to boost employee morale and personal growth.

Concern (Parker Leung):

However, I can see how some employees might feel overwhelmed by the idea of adding more to their workload. It could be tough to balance work and learning.

Denim Outfits on Fridays Policy

Positive (Samantha Lee):

As a creative person I would love to wear clothes that best represent me and reflect my unique style. This new policy will undoubtedly enhance my creativity and work efficiency.

Positive (Vincent Chan):

I couldn't agree more. Dressing in denim on Fridays allows us to express our unique style, fostering a positive work environment. This new policy is a perfect match for my wardrobe and allows me to showcase my personal style in the office.

Flexi Lunch Break Policy

Positive (Gladys Yeung):

The new policy allows me to have my lunch earlier so that I can refresh myself for the afternoon work. More importantly, I find myself empowered to make the decisions about my own schedules.

Concern (Edith Li):

I agree that the policy could improve employees' productivity and sense of belonging. What I concern the most is the difficulty in coordinating with colleagues. When lunch breaks become flexible, it could lead to scheduling conflicts or disruptions in workflow.

Remote Office Policy

Positive (Alex Cheung):

This policy allows me to have more flexibility to be in close proximity with my family. I believe this will enable me to be more focused in delivering quality work without having to worry about my family.

Positive (Jamie Chau):

Not only can it improve our staff morale but I think it could decrease the turnover rate in the long run. It'll also be easier to manage work-life balance. Not to mention the policy itself would save us a lot of commute time for those who live afar, allowing us to be less tired from commuting. It'll also be easier to manage work life balance as well.
